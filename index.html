Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about shopping, payments, selling, and delivery on DavinciMarket.

What is DavinciMarket?

DavinciMarket is an online marketplace where shoppers can discover products from independent sellers.

How do I place an order?

Browse the shop, add items to your cart, then continue to checkout and enter your delivery details.

How do I pay for an order?

Checkout provides OPay bank transfer details. After placing your order, WhatsApp opens so you can send your payment receipt. The order remains pending until an administrator confirms the payment.

How is delivery arranged?

Each seller arranges delivery directly with the buyer. Delivery fees and timing are agreed separately with the seller.

How can I sell on DavinciMarket?

Create a seller account, sign in to the seller centre, and submit your products. Products appear in the shop after administrator approval.

When will my order be confirmed?

Your order is confirmed after the administrator verifies your bank transfer. The seller can then contact you to arrange delivery.
